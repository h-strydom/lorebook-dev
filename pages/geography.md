The lands of Veldmere hug a single long coast, with the Hollow Sea on one side and the smoking hills on the other. Almost everyone lives within a day's walk of a bell.

![A map of the Veldmere coast, with the Cinder Highlands in the north-east and the Hollow Sea in the west](blob:sha256-88c39575eb3b2a13240fe70a3d7d8ff0159cd155b1a4b0a3f9db27f343955f67 "The coast as the Bellwardens chart it. North is up.")

## Regions

- [[The Marrow Coast]] is the long strip of towns behind the sea wall.
- [[The Cinder Highlands]] are the old volcanic uplands where [[Karrowdeep]] keeps its forges.
- [[The Drowned Archive]] lies under the sea, and most people say it does not exist.

:::info{title="Travel"}
A walker covers about 12 leagues a day on the Glass Road and 8 on the Marrow's sand tracks. Add a day for every bell crossing, because the wardens hold the road shut from the dusk bell to the dawn bell.
:::
