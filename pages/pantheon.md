Veldmere's faith is plain and practical. There are two powers, and one of them is only barely a god.

- [[The Lantern Mother]], the keeper of light and roads, worshipped by lamp and word.
- [[The Drowned Brother]], who is not worshipped but is thanked at the end of a long voyage.
- [[The Three Moons]], which the tide-priests count as three small gods or as one large clock.
