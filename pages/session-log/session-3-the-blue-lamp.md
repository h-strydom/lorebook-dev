**In the world:** 8 Highsun. The company returns to [[Saltgate]] with the *Patient Margaret*'s log and finds the blue lamp burning under the Underpier.

## What happened

1. [[Sister Ilse of the Lanterns|Sister Ilse]] refused to explain the lamp, and then did.
2. [[Odric Vane]] led them down the stair nobody mentions.
3. They found [[Pell Threadwick]]'s drawings on the wall, thirty of them, all of the same figure.

:::info{title="Hook"}
One of the drawings has a date in the corner: the twentieth of Harvestide, the day of the Bell Mass.
:::
