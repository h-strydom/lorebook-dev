**In the world:** 4 to 6 Highsun. The company travels the sand tracks to [[Port Haldane]] and back, following a rumour about a ship that came in without a lamp.

## What happened

1. At Port Haldane they found the ship, a grain barge named the *Patient Margaret*, empty and cold.
2. Its crew had gone ashore, according to the harbourmaster. No one had seen them.
3. A [[Brinewraith]] was found in the barge's hold, standing very still.
4. The company spoke to the Court's judge, who said nothing useful and charged for it.

:::info{title="Clue"}
The barge's log ends with the words "the third bell is not a bell". The company noticed it; nobody has said it aloud.
:::
