**In the world:** 3 Highsun. The company arrives in [[Saltgate]] at the end of a long road, just as the dawn bell rings. It rings twice.

## What happened

1. The company reached the Wallside and met [[Marta Quillon]], who had been up all night.
2. She told them the dawn bell has never rung twice in the town's history.
3. The company asked questions at the [[The Bellhouse|Bellhouse]] and were turned away by Hesper the keeper.
4. [[Odric Vane]] offered to sell them a tide table and a story, in that order.

## Notes

The company decided to stay a week and take Marta's commission.

:::secret{title="What was really going on"}
The second ring was not the dawn bell. It was the Seventh Bell, and it rang once. Marta heard it as the dawn bell's echo because she has never heard the Seventh Bell, and she cannot admit to herself that she has.
:::
