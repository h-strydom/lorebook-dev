Veldmere's history is told in two halves: *before the Sundering*, when the lowlands were dry and the kings were many, and *after*, when the sea was a neighbour and the bells were a wage.

- [[The Sundering]], the day the world broke.
- [[The Age of Bells]], the long era of walls and wardens.
- The [[Reckoning of Years|calendar]] counts the years, with three eras.

The full dates are on the timeline.
