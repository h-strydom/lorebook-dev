The Marrow is a strip of reclaimed shore forty leagues long and never more than six wide. Behind it the hills rise quickly; in front of it the Hollow Sea breathes in and out twice a day with a force the older towns still call "the pull".

Saltgate is the largest town and the seat of the [[Bellwardens]]. South of it the sand tracks run to [[Port Haldane]], the only deep harbour, and then dwindle into [[Dunmoor Fen]], where the land has never quite decided to be land.

## Life behind the wall

The sea wall is the region's spine. Every household within it pays the salt tithe, and every child learns the bell signals before they learn their letters: one stroke for the turn of the tide, two for a ship in sight, three for the sea coming over.

> The wall does not keep the sea out. The wall keeps the sea *polite*.
>
> Proverb of the Marrow

## What grows

Samphire, eelgrass, small stubborn barley and a great deal of salt. Fishing is the second industry and smuggling, whatever the wardens say, is the third.

:::warning{title="Rough weather"}
Between Mistfall and Longnight the fog bells ring all day. Travellers who ignore them have a habit of walking into the sea.
:::

:::::secret{title="What the pull really is"}
The pull is not the tide. It is the [[The Drowned Brother]] leaning on the shore, and it has been getting stronger by a hair every year since the Salt War.

::::info{title="If the party asks a priest"}
The priests of the [[The Lantern Mother]] will say the sea is simply hungry, and that bells are the oldest remedy for hunger.
::::
