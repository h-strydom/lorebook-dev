The first and only chamber visitors have described, a round room with a floor of wet slate and a single table. The air smells of ink and low tide.

On the table lies an open ledger. It is always open at the page of the visitor's own name.

> It listed my name, my birth, my parents' names, the date I would drown, and a margin note: *not yet*.
>
> The only account, from a salvager who would not give her own name
