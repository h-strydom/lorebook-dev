Saltgate sits where the Marrow's only river reaches the sea, which is why it has a wall higher than any other and a harbour mouth you could throw a stone across. It is a town of about four thousand people, nearly all of them related to someone in the [[The Bellwardens|Bellwardens]], the harbour guild, or both.

## Customs

Each dusk the dusk bell closes the harbour chain; each dawn the dawn bell opens it. Lately the town has begun to count the strokes aloud, and nobody says why.

:::info{title="Rumour"}
Sailors say a blue light has been seen under the pier at the turn of the tide. Nobody has said whose lamp it is.
:::

The oldest document in town is the [[id:01M7D3N1XA0WENBW91NBD08Q2V|Tidebell Charter]], which still hangs in the Bellhouse porch.

## Weather

Saltgate is windy for nine months of the year and foggy for the other three. The wardens ring the fog bell every quarter hour from Mistfall to Longnight.
