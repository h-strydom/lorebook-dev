Port Haldane has the only harbour on the Marrow deep enough for the big grain ships, and it has never forgiven Saltgate for having the wall. It is a town of wharves and warehouses, loud and colder than Saltgate and a good deal more honest about its vices.

The harbourmaster, a retired Tidebound judge, keeps the port's one rule: no ship leaves owing money. Everything else is negotiable.

## Trade

- Grain from the south, in sacks marked with the shipper's glyph.
- Salt fish, dried and smoked, going inland.
- Glass from the Highlands, carried down the [[The Cinder Highlands|Glass Road]].
- Everything the wardens would rather not count, passing through the [[Odric Vane|Underpier]] and then up the coast.

:::warning
Do not take a berth on a ship that has no lamp on its masthead. Port Haldane has a long memory for ships that came in dark and did not leave.
:::
