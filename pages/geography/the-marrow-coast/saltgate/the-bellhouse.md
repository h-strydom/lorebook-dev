A squat tower of black basalt, forty feet across and sixty high, with seven bells hung in its crown. It was the first building raised in Saltgate and the last the sea would ever take.

## The bells

1. **The Turner**, rung once at each tide.
2. **The Hail**, for ships.
3. **The Warning**, three strokes, never rung in jest.
4. **The Dusk Bell** and **The Dawn Bell**, which close and open the harbour.
5. **The Mass Bell**, rung at the Bell Mass on the twentieth of Harvestide.
6. **The Seventh Bell**, which has no clapper, and which the wardens will not discuss.

## Visiting

Anyone may enter the porch. The stair is for wardens only, and the keeper, a stooped woman named Hesper, takes the rule very seriously.

The Seventh Bell is not silent. On the nights when the sea comes closest it hums, and the hum has a tune, and the tune is the first line of the hymn the [[The Hollow Choir|Choir]] sings.
