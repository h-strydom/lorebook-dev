The Marrow has three ways in: the coast road, the river and the sea. Each has a toll, and each toll has a story about who is allowed to skip it.

::::columns
:::column
### By road

The coast road runs from [[Haldane Harbour]] to [[Saltgate]] in four days on foot, three by cart. Waystones every league carry a bell-mark; a missing mark means the stone was moved.

- Toll at the Fen Bridge: one copper a wheel
- Wardens ride it twice a month
:::
:::column
### By river

Flat barges come down from [[Karrowdeep]] with glass and ore. The bargefolk keep their own reckoning of days and are never in a hurry.

- Toll at the Salt Lock: a tenth of the cargo
- No barges move on [[The Three Moons|Twin Lights]] nights
:::
