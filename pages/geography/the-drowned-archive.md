Nobody agrees where it is. The oldest map puts it two leagues off the Marrow Coast; a smuggler's rhyme puts it under the Bellhouse itself. What everyone agrees on is that it holds the names of everyone the sea has taken since the Sundering, written in a hand that does not fade.

The Archive appears to be a building only from above. From within it is a vast, dry library with no walls, and the shelves go on past the point where a lantern's light gives out.

:::secret{title="Entry"}
There is one way in: ring the Seventh Bell on the night of the dark moon with a name written on a strip of bark. The sea will open the stair. It will want a name in return, and it is not particular whose.
:::
