The Highlands are what the world looked like before the sea arrived: black stone, hard rain and a sky the colour of old iron. Three volcanoes still smoke on clear days, and the whole range rings softly in the evenings as the rock cools.

The people here do not ring bells. They hammer. Every hold keeps a forge going, and the sound of the forges is, to the Highlanders, the proper answer to the sea.

## Places

- [[Karrowdeep]], the great hold, whose gates have been shut for a generation.
- The Glass Road, a ribbon of fused sand some thirty leagues long that was laid, they say, by a single lightning strike. It is the only dry road to the coast.

:::info{title="Climate"}
Cold at night, brutally so; sunny and dry by day. Water is sold by the cup above the second terrace.
:::
