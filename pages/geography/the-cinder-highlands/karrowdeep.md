A city carved down into the mountain rather than built on it, Karrowdeep has nine levels above the Great Forge and, they say, nine below. Only the upper five are known to outsiders.

The hold is ruled by a Council of Hammers, each seat held by a master smith chosen by lot from the guild's best. They are fair, slow and extremely stubborn. A request made to Karrowdeep takes three months to answer, and the answer is generally yes.

## Trade and trouble

Karrowdeep sells blades, tools and a little glass to the coast, and buys salt. The balance has been tipping for years: the coast needs less iron and the hold needs more salt, and nobody in either place understands why.

:::secret{title="The sealed gates"}
Karrowdeep sealed its lower gates in AB 399 after a smith heard the third bell through nine hundred feet of rock. The Council has never told the coast. They are waiting to see whether the sound comes again.
:::
