A sailor's god, of sorts: the one who stayed behind when the Sundering took the lowlands. The coast does not pray to him. It thanks him, quietly, at the end of a safe voyage, the way you might thank a creditor who has not yet called in the debt.

The tide-priests teach that he sleeps under the Hollow Sea and that the pull of the tide is his breathing. The bells, they say, are a lullaby.

## Servants

His servants are the things that wash up on the Marrow after storms, the [[Brinewraith]] chief among them. They are not evil; they are *owed*.

:::secret
The Brother is awake. He has been awake since the Salt War. The bells do not lull him; they *bargain* with him, and every stroke sold a little more of the coast.
:::
