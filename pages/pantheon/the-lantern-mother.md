The Lantern Mother is the goddess of every lit window. She does not command, bless or smite; she *keeps*. Her faithful believe that every lamp tended with care is a small gift to her, and that she remembers every one.

## Practice

Her worship has no temples, only lamps. A new lamplighter lights their first lamp from another's flame and promises to do the same for a stranger. Her day is the last night of the old year, when every lamp on the coast is lit at once and left burning until dawn.

## Symbols

A lantern, an open hand, a flame without a wick.

:::info{title="Common blessing"}
*May your lamp be lit when you return.*
:::
