Veldmere has three moons, and the tide-priests keep a careful count of them.

| Moon | Habits |
| --- | --- |
| Orrin | Regular: a 28-night round that the wardens use to set the tide tables |
| Veyra | Wanders: dark for forty to sixty nights, then full for three. Always full on the fifteenth of Highsun |
| The Cinder Eye | Always the same, small and red and never changing |

Veyra's wandering is the coast's great uncertainty: nobody can say when she will return, only that she will be full for her feast day.

:::info
The calendar page tracks all three. Players see today's phases exactly, and for the nights ahead only the frames of days Veyra may fall in.
:::

:::secret{title="Why Veyra is late"}
Veyra is not wandering. She is *following* the Seventh Bell, and every time it hums she is pulled a little closer to the sea.
:::
