A thin, tidy man in his fifties who the [[id:01M7D3MYMBEXDJ9JXM4P233FWB|Choir]] calls "the Cantor" and the rest of Saltgate has forgotten. He was a Bellwarden once, the youngest bell-keeper in a century, and he is the reason the Bell-Warden's chair has stood empty for eleven years.

:::secret{title="What he did"}
Corvin discovered, in AB 478, that every stroke of the Turner bell is paid for in a name taken from the Drowned Archive. He tried to stop the bells. The wardens drove him out. He has spent the years since teaching the Choir that the only honest way to stop paying is to ring the Seventh Bell.

::::warning{title="He is not a villain"}
He is right about the debt. He is wrong about the remedy. Play him as a man who has done the arithmetic and cannot unlearn it.
::::
:::

Since the Blue Lamp affair Corvin drinks at the Gull and Kettle on Lamp Row, and pays in old Tidebound coin.
