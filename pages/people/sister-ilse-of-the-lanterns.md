The senior [[Guild of Lamplighters|Lamplighter]] of Saltgate, seventy years old, silver-haired and about as soft as a keel. She has lit the lamps of Lamp Row every evening for fifty-four years and has never missed one, including the night she broke her hip.

![Portrait of Sister Ilse, an older woman with silver hair](blob:sha256-da5e3fc737cf5b8e7817340d98191711910b2793b496a37aade82283ab9c8ebd "Sister Ilse, in the guildhall light.")

## What she knows

Everything about the town's quiet corners: who is dying, who is lying, who owes oil. She also knows the one lamp in Saltgate that burns blue and has never told anyone where.

## Manner

Calm, kind and unyielding. She will feed you, house you and refuse to tell you a thing, all in the same breath.

> A lamp is a promise to the next person. If you cannot keep it, do not light it.
>
> Sister Ilse, to every new apprentice
