Captain of the Wall at Saltgate, fifty-one, broad as a door and quieter than one. She came up through the ranks of the [[The Bellwardens|wardens]] by being the person who was still awake when the bell had to be rung.

![Portrait of Marta Quillon, a broad woman in a blue hat](blob:sha256-27265275412f905449cf54af05451edf0a10f0204c066ba2a8cbf7157c327ecd "Captain Marta Quillon, in the wall's colours.")

## Manner

Soft-spoken and exact. She remembers names, debts and weather. She has never been seen to lose her temper, and the wardens who served under her will tell you, unprompted, that this is the frightening part.

## What she wants

To see the Bell-Warden's chair filled before she retires, and to find out who has been stealing sleep from her wardens. She believes there is a person behind the trouble. She is right.

## In play

- She hires outsiders when she cannot ask her own wardens, and pays them at the end of the job, never the start.
- She will pay in silver and favours, never in secrets.
- She trusts [[Sister Ilse of the Lanterns]] and does not trust [[Odric Vane]], though she has used him.

:::secret{title="Her private ledger"}
Marta keeps a small book listing every warden who has gone missing on the night watch. There are sixty-one names. She has told nobody, because she does not know whether telling would help.
:::
