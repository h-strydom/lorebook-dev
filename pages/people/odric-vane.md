A slim, cheerful man of about forty who runs the Underpier in [[Saltgate]] like an inn that happens to sell everything. He wears a red scarf, smokes a clay pipe and is, by his own account, merely a businessman with unusual customers.

![Portrait of Odric Vane, a man in a red headscarf](blob:sha256-7700687243eab080058cb8347bd8522e80956738f1d6869b93fb3126f2663d49 "Odric, who says he is not a smuggler.")

## What he sells

Whatever you need, once, at a fair price and with a story attached. He knows the tunnels under the wall, the tide tables to the minute and every ship that has come in without a lamp.

## Quirks

- Calls everyone "friend", including enemies.
- Never touches the bell-rope, the wall or the tithe.
- Owes a great deal to the [[The Tidebound Court|Court]] and has bribed three of its judges.

:::info{title="Reputation"}
Honest in small things and flexible in large ones. The [[The Bellwardens|wardens]] tolerate him because he reports problems before they become emergencies.
:::
