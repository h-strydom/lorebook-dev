Eight years old, the daughter of a net-mender, and the only person in Saltgate who has drawn the Seventh Bell. Her drawings are on the walls of the net loft: bells, boats and a tall, thin figure standing in the shallows where nobody ever stands.

She does not talk much. When she does, she is usually right and sometimes frightening.

## In play

- Offers the party a drawing instead of an answer.
- Is escorted everywhere by a one-eyed dog named Button.
- Is under the protection of [[Sister Ilse of the Lanterns|Sister Ilse]] and, unofficially, of the whole of Lamp Row.
