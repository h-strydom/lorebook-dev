Three powers matter on the coast, and a fourth would rather you did not know it was a power. They agree on very little except that the bells must ring.

- [[The Bellwardens]] keep the wall, the bells and the peace.
- The [[Guild of Lamplighters]] keep the lights, the roads and the gossip.
- [[The Tidebound Court]] keep the law, and charge for it.

| Faction | Seat | Strength | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bellwardens | [[Saltgate]] | Discipline and the wall | Few in number, tired |
| Lamplighters | Lamp Row | Information | Faithful to a fault |
| Tidebound Court | [[Haldane Harbour]] | Money and writs | Slow, and hated |
