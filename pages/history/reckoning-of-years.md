Veldmere counts days in a five-day week (Tern, Oar, Keel, Mast and Sail) and years in months of uneven length. Nine months, and three days of Wintermark between the old year and the new.

The full calendar, with moons, festivals and the current date, is on the Calendar page of this project.

:::info{title="Festivals"}
The Bell Mass is on 20 Harvestide; Veyra's feast on 15 Highsun; the Lantern Night, when every lamp on the coast is lit, on the first day of Wintermark.
:::
