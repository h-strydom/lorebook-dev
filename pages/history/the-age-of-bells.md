The Age of Bells begins with the casting of the first Tidebell in Port Haldane and the Bell Accord that followed. It was an age of walls: walls against the sea, walls against the dark, walls between the towns that had once been countries.

## Character

It was an age of caution. People measured everything: the tide, the tithe, the length of the wall, the number of strokes a warden could ring before their arm gave out. It was also an age of quiet, good manners. Those who lived through it describe it as the first time anyone could count on the morning.

:::info{title="Eras in the calendar"}
The Age of Bells is "AB" in the calendar, and runs for five hundred years. The Rekindling begins the "RL" era, the Reckoning of Lamps, which is the present.
:::
