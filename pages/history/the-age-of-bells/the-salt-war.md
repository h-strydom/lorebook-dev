A six-year conflict between the coastal towns and the Highland holds, fought over the price of salt and, underneath that, over who held the right to ring the bells.

## Causes

The Highlands needed salt to cure meat and quench steel. The coast needed iron for bells and tools. Both sides raised the price, then raised it again, then closed the Glass Road.

## The course of the war

1. **Year one.** The road closes. Prices triple.
2. **Years two to four.** Skirmishes; Karrowdeep raids the coastal salt-pans.
3. **Year five.** The coast rings every bell at once for a night and a day. The sea comes over the Highland ford.
4. **Year six.** The holds sue for peace.

:::secret{title="The night of all the bells"}
The "sea over the ford" was not an accident of tide. The wardens rang all seven bells, including the Seventh, and the Brother answered. The ford drowned seven hundred people. The coast has called it a victory ever since.
:::
