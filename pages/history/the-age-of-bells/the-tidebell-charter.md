The founding document of Saltgate's Bellwardens, sealed in AB 412 and kept in a glass case in the Bellhouse porch. It sets out the rights, duties and tithes of the order in three short articles.

:file[Tidebell Charter.pdf]{src="blob:sha256-2469f5d2786d0a7ff6258be8a737bf1bb44d3eb0b3cbc3c4fa854ee99b864ee5"}

## Summary

1. The wardens keep the wall and the bells.
2. Every household pays one salt tithe.
3. A false bell strikes the warden from the roll; a true warning is never false.

## Notes

The charter's last clause, about false bells, has been used twice to remove wardens and never once to protect one. Scholars of the order argue that it was written to be used only against the guilty and used, in practice, against the inconvenient.

:::warning
The original is fragile. The wardens will let you read the copy, but not touch the glass.
:::
