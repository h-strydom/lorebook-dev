Saltgate was founded in AB 14 by a salt-boiler named Oswin Tarrow, a retired warden named Mab, and eleven families from the drowned city of Haldane. They chose the site because the river mouth was narrow and because a bell had been found in the shallows, ringing.

## The first winter

They lived in boats. The wall was a pile of rocks and the bell hung from a mast. Oswin rang it every tide, and the sea stayed out.

By the spring they had a wall of sand and rock four feet high; by the next they had one of ten.

The [[The Bellhouse|Bellhouse]] was raised around the bell over eight years, finished in AB 22, and the first charter was written then, in a hand that is still legible.
