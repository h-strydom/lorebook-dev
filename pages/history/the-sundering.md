On the twelfth day of Longnight, in the last year of the old reckoning, the ground under the lowland kingdoms dropped. It dropped in an afternoon. By dusk the Hollow Sea had come in over four hundred leagues of fields, cities and roads, and by dawn it had stopped.

Nobody knows why it stopped. Nobody knows why it began. The survivors, those who had been on the heights, found the new coast already humming.

## What the records agree on

1. The ground fell, quickly and without fire.
2. The sea rose behind it, calmly.
3. Three bells cast the year before in the city of Haldane were found in the surf, unbroken and ringing.

## What they do not

Whether the sea was sent, summoned or *called*. A bell-keeper once suggested the last. [[id:01M7D3MZQ30N0A5XJREZAY2ZFN|He was struck from the rolls]] for the suggestion.

> We did not lose the land. We were told to leave it.
>
> Attributed to a survivor of Haldane, BS 1
