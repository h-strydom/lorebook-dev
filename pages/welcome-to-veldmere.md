Veldmere is a thin country between a hungry sea and a smoking mountain, held together by bells, lamps and habit. This is the *showcase world* of Lorebook: an invented setting with a little of everything the wiki can do, so you can click around before you write your own.

## Today

The company is in [[Saltgate]], where the dawn bell rang twice and nobody can say why. [[Marta Quillon]] wants answers, and a good many people would rather she did not have them.

:::info{title="How this world is built"}
Every page here is a plain text file in a git repository. Notes like this one are visible to everyone; warnings are too; and the violet notes marked as secret are for the admin only and never leave the server for a player.
:::
