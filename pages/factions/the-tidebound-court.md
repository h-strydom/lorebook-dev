The Court is Veldmere's law. It sits in Port Haldane, in a grain exchange it never bothered to rename, and its judges are chosen by exam, bribe or inheritance in about equal proportions. They hear the cases of every town between the Highlands and the fen, and they charge by the day.

There are no prisons. The Court fines, bonds, exiles or, in the worst cases, *binds*: the guilty are bound by oath to the sea, which in practice means they may not step on the Marrow's sand again.

![The Tidebound Court's crest, three silver waves](blob:sha256-cade79cfbebfb98757cc65764c74238fdcdf814b6b0dada8a0b82174b0f84b59 "Three waves, one verdict.")

## Procedure

1. Complaint is lodged at the exchange with a fee.
2. Both sides are summoned, and each pays the judges' day-rate.
3. The judge hears the case in one day, or says it needs more days.
4. The verdict is written and the *price of the verdict* is written beside it.

> Justice is always available. It is merely not always affordable.
>
> Motto of the Court, as quoted by its critics

## Relations

The Court despises the [[The Bellwardens|wardens]] for acting without writs and fears the Lamplighters for knowing which judges took which bribes. It is the only faction that has never rung a bell.
