The wardens are an order of four hundred men and women who have sworn to keep the bells ringing on time and the sea wall standing. They wear grey coats with a bronze bell at the collar, and they are very, very tired.

Their rule is simple and old: *ring the bell, hold the wall, count the cost later.* The wardens are poorly paid and well respected, a combination that keeps good people and keeps them cynical.

![The crest of the Bellwardens](blob:sha256-c8150deedcad52c7571f3f20f77ca0c42a87abd7ed27edb695ce308ea15eecf5 "Blue field, bronze bell.")

## Ranks

1. Wall-hand, the common warden.
2. Bell-keeper, who tends one bell of the seven.
3. Captain, who holds a stretch of the wall. [[Marta Quillon]] holds Saltgate's.
4. The Bell-Warden, a post empty for eleven years.

## What they do

They patrol the wall, ring the bells on schedule, collect the salt tithe and keep the [[id:01M7D3N1XA0WENBW91NBD08Q2V|charter]]. They also, in their quiet way, look after the poor of Saltgate, who have nowhere else to go.

:::warning{title="Do not cross the wardens"}
A warden who is insulted is within their rights to ask you to leave the town. A warden who is *wronged* may ask more.
:::

:::secret{title="The dwindling"}
There were nine hundred wardens in AB 400. The order is dying because the pull is getting stronger and wardens who stand the night watch on the wall come back changed. [[Marta Quillon]] has begun to keep a private count of the ones who have not come back at all.
:::
