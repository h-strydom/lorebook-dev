Every road in Veldmere has lamps at its crossings, and every lamp is lit and trimmed by a lamplighter. This was a trade once. Now it is a faith, a guild and the nearest thing the coast has to a postal service.

They are devoted to [[The Lantern Mother]], and their sisters and brothers hold that no lamp may ever be allowed to go out unattended. They carry news between the towns, trade rumours for oil and keep a vast, informal ledger of who owes what to whom.

![The Lamplighters' crest, a glass lantern on brown](blob:sha256-f092056f698a9a18af750ae36b3aeeb71cd0db5aebe16a84aac4d498781e2121 "A lantern, always lit.")

## The Schism

In AB 331 the guild split over whether a lamp may burn *blue*. The orthodox said blue light was a sign of the sea's regard; the reformers said it was a sign the oil had been drowned. The reformers lost. Their descendants still light blue lamps in cellars.

:::info{title="Lamp codes"}
A white lamp is a welcome; a yellow lamp is a warning; a red lamp is a quarantine; a blue lamp means *do not come near*, or so the reformers claim.
:::

Their guildhall on Lamp Row is open to travellers who bring a lamp's worth of oil and are willing to be recorded. [[Sister Ilse of the Lanterns|Sister Ilse]] is the ranking Lamplighter in Saltgate.
