A quiet congregation of about thirty, meeting under [[id:01M7D3MZQ30N0A5XJREZAY2ZFN|a patron the town has forgotten]], who believe the sea is not an enemy but a debt, and that a debt unpaid grows.

They sing in cellars, in rowboats at slack tide and once, famously, in the hold of a ship that never reached port. Their hymn has seven lines, one for each bell of the Bellhouse.

:::secret{title="What they want"}
To ring the Seventh Bell. The Choir believes that when the seventh bell is rung the pull will stop, and the Drowned Brother will take what he is owed from those who agreed to pay: themselves. They may be right. They have not yet considered who else the Brother counts as having agreed.
:::
